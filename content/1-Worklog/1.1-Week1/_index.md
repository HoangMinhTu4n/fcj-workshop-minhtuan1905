### Week 1 Objectives:

* Get familiar with members of the First Cloud AI Journey program.
* Learn the internship rules, regulations and procedures.
* Get an overview of AWS and its main service groups (Compute, Storage, Networking, Database).
* Get familiar with AWS Management Console and install, configure AWS CLI.
* Learn Amazon EC2 basics (Instance Types, AMI, EBS, Elastic IP) and SSH connections.
