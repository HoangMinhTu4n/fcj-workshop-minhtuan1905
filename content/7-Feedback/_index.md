### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The working environment is friendly and open. Members of the FCAJ community are always ready to lend a hand whenever I run into difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, which helps me concentrate better. That said, I believe more networking or team-building activities would help everyone get to know one another even better.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
The mentor gave me thorough guidance, patiently explained things whenever I was confused, and constantly encouraged me to ask questions. The admin team took care of the procedures, documents, and conditions I needed so I could work smoothly. What I appreciated most was that the mentor let me try to solve problems on my own instead of simply handing me the answers.

**3. Relevance of the Work to My Academic Background**
The tasks I was assigned matched what I had studied at university, while also opening up new areas I had never explored before — especially cloud computing and serverless architecture. This allowed me to reinforce my foundation while picking up practical skills at the same time.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**
During the internship, I picked up many new skills, including designing serverless architectures, working with AWS Step Functions, writing infrastructure as code, and building CI/CD pipelines. The mentor also shared plenty of real-world experience, which helped me define my career direction more clearly.

**5. Company Culture and Team Spirit**
The culture at the company is very positive: people respect one another, work seriously, and still keep a friendly atmosphere. When an urgent project comes up, everyone works together and supports each other regardless of their role. As an intern, this made me feel like a true part of the team.

**6. Internship Policy and Benefits**
The company provides an internship allowance and flexible working hours when needed. In addition, taking part in internal training sessions and hands-on workshops was a big advantage.

### Personal Reflections After the Internship

I believe the program offered a clear learning roadmap together with dedicated support from the mentor team. Many sharing sessions were held by Cloud Engineers and Solution Architects working in enterprises, giving students a wealth of knowledge and practical experience.

After completing the FCAJ Bootcamp, I gained many valuable lessons — from technical knowledge to equally important soft skills such as teamwork, progress management, searching for technical documentation, presenting ideas, and solving problems. These experiences have made me more confident as I get ready to step into the workforce after graduation.

I would gladly recommend the FCAJ Bootcamp to students interested in Cloud Computing, DevOps, or AI, because it offers a high-quality learning environment with practical content and strong support from the community.

In the future, I hope to join FCAJ's advanced programs on AWS Solution Architecture and DevOps on AWS to keep expanding my knowledge and skills. I also hope to give back to the FCAJ community through academic activities and real-world projects.

Finally, I would like to sincerely thank the mentors, the Admin Team, and the organizing committee of the First Cloud AI Journey Bootcamp for guiding, supporting, and accompanying me throughout the internship. This experience has helped me grow in professional knowledge, work skills, and problem-solving mindset.

In particular, I want to express my deep gratitude to Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung — Head of Solutions Architecture / SA Director at AWS Vietnam — who founded and led the FCAJ community, a place where I had the chance to learn, grow, and explore many new technologies. His guidance, practical experience, and support helped me see more clearly which areas I needed to improve, inspiring me to keep learning and bettering myself every day.
